# Domain Concepts & Invariants

Ubiquitous domain language, business rules, and entity relationships for this project.

## Core Entities & Vocabulary

- **[EntityName]**: One-sentence definition of what this entity represents.
  - _Invariants_: Mandatory business rules or lifecycle constraints.
  - _Avoid_: Terms or synonyms that cause confusion.

## Domain Relationships

- **[EntityA] → [EntityB]**: Nature of the interaction or event stream.
